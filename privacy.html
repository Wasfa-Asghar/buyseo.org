Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains what BuySEO collects to run group buy tool access, how that information is used, and how you can reach us about privacy requests.

2. Information we store

We typically store your email address, name, and phone number, plus order history and support messages needed to deliver and maintain access. We do not store full card numbers on BuySEO pages — payment processors handle card data at checkout.

3. How we use information

We use account details to create memberships, activate tools, process renewals, prevent abuse (such as sharing or multi-IP misuse), and answer support requests on WhatsApp, email, or phone.

4. Tool sessions

Access is delivered through our dashboard and, where required, a browser extension. Session isolation is designed so your work stays in your environment. We do not sell personal information to third parties for marketing lists.

5. Cookies and security

We may use essential cookies for login and security, and optional analytics to understand how the public site is used so we can improve it.

6. Retention

We keep account and order records for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal or billing requirements, and resolve disputes. You may request updates or deletion where the law allows and where we are not required to retain records.
