FAQ

1. Will SEO group buy work for me?

BuySEO is built for startups, small businesses, freelancers, and agencies that need SEO, PPC, e-commerce, and research tools without paying full retail for every license. If you want better rankings, cleaner PPC research, or competitor insights, group buy access through BuySEO is designed for that use case.

2. What other options are there and how much do they cost?

Buying tools one by one at retail adds up fast. A single platform can run roughly $60–$250 a month, and flagship SEO suites often sit between about $99 and $999+ per month. Most serious workflows need several tools at once, so a solo stack can become a heavy recurring cost. BuySEO consolidates member-priced access so you are not forced to carry five full retail invoices.

3. Are there any limitations?

Yes. Member pricing is lower because capacity is shared fairly across customers. API access, some bulk workflows, and certain export features may be limited or disabled so everyone gets usable access. After you sign up, open the Limits section in your member menu for the current fair-use rules on your plan and tools.

4. How does this service work?

Create your account, complete checkout, then open the BuySEO / seogroupsbuy member area. Follow the short video guide inside the dashboard and launch tools from there — no cracked software and no shared vendor passwords for most products.

5. How long does it take to activate my account?

Checkout is mostly automated. Access usually starts within about two minutes after payment clears. Some PayPal payments may need a short manual check before tools unlock.

6. Do I need to install software, a Chrome extension, or RDP?

Most members only need our browser extension to open tools. Some tools open with no extension at all. You do not need RDP software to use BuySEO.

7. Is your service available on Mac?

Yes. BuySEO works on Windows, macOS, ChromeOS, and major Linux distributions through popular browsers such as Chrome and Firefox. Many tools also work on iOS and Android in a supported browser.

8. Do you offer refunds?

If a purchased single tool stays down and no working update is delivered within 24 hours, you can request a full refund for that tool. Refunds do not apply to Lite, Agency, or Advance-style package plans where we guarantee the pack remains usable as a whole. See the full Refund Policy for details.

9. Can I share my account with my team, friends, or family?

No. One membership is for one person. Sharing logins with teammates, friends, or family can lead to a permanent ban without notice.

10. What payment methods can I use?

You can pay with PayPal, major cards, and Google Pay at checkout. Other local methods may appear depending on the payment page shown at the time of purchase.

11. How is this so cheap? Is this a scam?

Group buying means many members fund shared premium seats together, so each person pays a fraction of retail. We buy official plans and deliver managed access — not pirated software. Try a small order first; if it is not useful for your workflow, you can stop and buy retail from the vendor instead. We only charge the published member price for the tools you select.

12. Can I use proxy or VPN IPs?

No. Proxies, VPNs, and RDPs used to access your membership can trigger a permanent ban. Use a normal home or office connection.

13. Can I use dynamic IPs?

Yes. Most broadband and mobile networks use dynamic IPs. Keep the connection consistent with your usual city or region and avoid hopping across distant locations.

14. Can I access my account from multiple places?

No. One account is for one person. Simultaneous logins from different IPs, states, or countries can get the account banned.

15. I see “Authentication Problem, please contact your website administrator” when logging in

That message usually means multiple-IP activity locked the account for about 24 hours. Avoid logging in from different networks, then try again after 24 hours.

16. Is any trial available before purchase?

Yes. A short trial (about one day) may be available at a member price or higher, depending on the offer shown at signup. Check the current trial details in the member area before you pay.

17. Do you share tool emails and passwords?

For almost all tools we do not share vendor emails or passwords. Access is delivered through the dashboard / extension. Rare exceptions are called out on the product page when they apply.

18. Extension shows “Seller ID disabled”?

Remove the extension, reinstall it from the member area instructions, then refresh and try again. That clears most Seller ID disabled cases.

19. Do you store my details?

We store basic account details needed to run the service — typically your email, name, and phone number — plus order and support history. Card data is handled by the payment processor. See our Privacy Policy.

20. How does the credit system work?

Credits are bonus balance you can earn on eligible recurring plans and spend on listed single tools in the member credits page on seogroupsbuy.com (BuySEO checkout).

  • Credits apply only to card payments — PayPal, EasyPaisa, and similar methods do not earn credits.
  • Credits start from the second month of recurring billing (the first month / trial cycle does not add credit).
  • Credits are available only on Lite, Agency, and Advance-style plans for recurring users. INR checkout does not receive credits.
  • Credits buy single tools only — they cannot redeem Lite, Agency, or Advance plans.
  • Credits expire 60 days after they are issued.
  • If you cancel recurring billing, you must wait through the restart cycle before credits resume.
  • Credits cannot be applied to tools that are already active on your account.

21. Why was my account permanently locked or banned?

Accounts may be banned permanently for reasons such as:

  • Abusive or foul language in chat
  • Repeated logins from different locations after warnings
  • Suspicious activity on the account
  • Sharing the account with friends, family, or a team after warnings
  • Threats of disputes or chargebacks (do not sign up if you disagree with our terms)

No refund is issued once an account is banned for policy violations.
