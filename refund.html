Refunds are not valid for Lite, Agency, Advance, or similar package plans. For those plans we guarantee that the pack remains usable as a whole; individual tools inside a pack may rotate or be limited under fair-use rules.

3. Used or working orders

Working tools and orders with confirmed usage are generally not refundable. Credits already issued or spent cannot be reversed as cash unless we confirm a billing error.
